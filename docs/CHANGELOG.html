v1.82 · 2026-10-26

Final damage composition

Pet attacks and learned player skills now show a final damage-type composition directly below their totals. All source packets ending in the same damage type are merged after conversion and bonuses; Acid, for example, appears once even when it came from several original types. Direct damage and damage over time per second have separate type tables, totals and composition bars. Mean shares are based on damage-range midpoints.

The same summary appears in the compact character panel, expanded skill dialog, pet page and pet skill previews. The original base/modifier/conversion/scaled calculation matrix remains available under Per-source calculations. Values are aggregated before display rounding; independent rounding may produce a one-cent display difference when manually adding rounded rows.

Basic weapon hits

Player basic weapon hits also show final composition per hand. Direct hits are displayed separately from DoT per second. Direct-damage DPS uses the mean direct hit multiplied by attack rate; DoT is not multiplied again by attack speed. Dual-wield hands and shield strikes remain separate, and unrelated skills, triggers and effects are not combined into a total build DPS.

Fixed flat pet damage additions being omitted from the upper bound when combined with native damage ranges; fixed additions now contribute to both bounds.

Compatibility and scope

This is a presentation-level aggregation of the existing final packets, not a second conversion or bonus calculation. Source probabilities, enemy defenses, crits and projectile overlap remain outside the static estimate. No character saves or game databases are modified. Build files, DB1 codes, links, metadata, language memory and celestial-rank policies stay compatible. v1.81 and earlier releases remain available.

English and Simplified Chinese release notes are included. Independent code remains MIT; existing game/mod, localization, third-party rights and Rem's project-specific permission are unchanged.

简体中文 · v1.82 · 2026-10-26

最终伤害构成

战宠攻击及角色已学技能在总伤害下增加最终类型构成。先完成转换和加成，再将最终类型相同的所有来源合并，例如多个原类型转换而来的酸性只列一行。直接伤害与每秒持续伤害分别列出各类型数值、合计及构成条；平均占比按伤害区间中点计算。

角色侧栏、技能明细弹窗、战宠页和技能中的战宠预览使用同一汇总。原基础/修正/转换/加成后的矩阵保留于“各来源计算明细”，默认收起。先聚合再做显示四舍五入；手工相加已取两位小数的行可能有0.01的显示误差。

普通武器攻击

角色普通武器攻击按单手展示最终构成，直接命中与每秒持续伤害分开。直接伤害DPS为直接伤害区间均值乘攻击频率，不再把持续伤害重复乘攻速。双持、盾牌、不同技能、触发及关联效果保持分开，不拼成虚构的构筑总DPS。

同时修正部分战宠固定伤害加成在与原生区间叠加时，上限漏加的问题；固定加成会同时进入上下限。

兼容与范围

本轮为现有最终伤害片段的展示汇总，不重复做转换或加成。触发概率、敌人防御、暴击和弹体重叠仍未纳入静态预估。不修改角色存档或游戏数据库；构筑文件、DB1、旧链接、资料、语言记忆和星座等级策略保持兼容。v1.81及旧版本保留。

随附中英双语更新说明；独立代码MIT及游戏、模组、汉化、第三方权利和Rem项目用途授权均不变。

v1.81 · 2026-10-25

Pet and player-scaling summon conversion

Pet damage now applies pet-only conversion before the destination damage-type bonuses. The pet page includes base, skill-modified, converted and pet-scaled damage, weapon portions, linked attacks, damage over time and conversion paths. Skill tooltip pet previews use the same model. Pet flat weapon bonuses do not become flat spell bonuses.

Player-scaling summons such as Blade Spirit now appear under Offense → Learned skill damage. PetPlayerScaling entities and their active internal attacks are resolved at the actual summon rank. They use player conversion and destination damage bonuses, never pet-only conversion or the player's weapon as their own. Local skill modifiers still apply according to the game's conversion order.

Conversion inventory

Offense and pet statistics now summarize their respective active global conversion. The expanded inventory separates player global, pet global, skill-specific and current-pet-specific properties, with named sources and equipment slots. Inactive or unlearned skill properties remain identifiable but are excluded from global totals. The currently selected weapon set is used. Competing conversion above 100% is normalized; Elemental is resolved into its component types. Unlocked item modifiers and set thresholds are respected.

Corrections and scope

Unassigned summon modifier slots no longer default to rank one. Missing and zero maximum-damage fields correctly fall back to their minimum values. Active summon-specific pet modifiers are resolved without enabling the unused modifier slots alongside them. Expanded source links include summon modifier records and their variants.

These are static per-effect estimates, not total DPS. Enemy mitigation, critical hits, hit chance, proc probability, projectile overlap, added retaliation and target-dependent health damage are not fully modeled. Missing active references are flagged; no substitute records or damage values are fabricated. Ordinary pet summon weapon/equipment tables and runtime behavior may still require in-game calibration. Rules are cross-checked against the official combat guide.

DB1, schema v1, old build files/links, celestial-rank import policies and language memory remain compatible. No character save or game database is modified. All v1.8 acquisition checks, Awakened equipment and dropItems=false exclusions are retained. Independent code remains MIT; game/mod, localization, third-party rights and Rem's project-specific permission are unchanged.

简体中文 · v1.81 · 2026-10-25

战宠与角色加成召唤物转换

战宠伤害先应用战宠专用转换，再按最终伤害类型加成。战宠页增加基础、技能修正、转换后、战宠加成后明细，区分武器部分、关联攻击、每秒持续伤害及转换路径；技能说明中的战宠预览共用同一模型。战宠的固定武器伤害加成不会无条件添加到纯法术中。

刀灵等角色伤害加成类召唤物现在进入“攻击→已学技能伤害”。正确识别PetPlayerScaling，并按实际召唤等级解析内部已激活攻击技能。它们读取角色转换与转换后类型加成，不读取战宠专用转换，也不会把玩家武器借作自身武器。技能局部修正仍按游戏转换顺序处理。

转换属性汇总

攻击及战宠属性页分别汇总当前生效的全局转换；展开可查看角色全局、战宠全局、技能专用及当前战宠自身转换，列出来源名称、部位、方向与比例。未启用增益和未学习技能的词条单独标识，不计入全局合计。按当前武器组统计。竞争转换超过100%时归一，元素按对应类型拆分；未解锁的套装修正不会生效。

修正与范围

修正召唤物未分配的物品修正槽位被默认按1级激活的问题。伤害最大值缺失或为零时正确回退到最小值；装备激活的召唤物专属修正独立生效，不连带启用其他预留修正。扩展召唤修正与变体记录关联。

仍为单次效果静态预估，不是实战总DPS；敌人减伤、暴击、命中率、触发概率、弹体重叠、反击附加及依赖目标生命的伤害未完整模拟。缺失的生效记录会提示，不补造数值。普通战宠自身武器/装备表及运行时行为仍可能需要游戏内校准。

DB1、schema v1、旧构筑文件/链接、星座等级导入策略与语言记忆保持兼容，不修改角色存档或游戏库。保留v1.8的获取性、觉醒装备及dropItems=false排除规则。独立代码MIT及游戏、模组、汉化、第三方权利和Rem项目用途授权均保持不变。

v1.8 · 2026-10-25

Catalogs and weapon families

Re-audited 145,867 merged database records. Compared with the v1.71 baseline, 1,237 catalog records are restored and 1,242 are now flagged unavailable in the normal catalog. This includes crafting skill runes and Eternal equipment outside the old records/items path restriction. Non-installable reward cards, crafting-only exchange materials, pet-only weapons, inactive affixes and cosmetic hairstyles are excluded from normal build selection. Creature equipment/loot references with dropItems=false are not acquisition sources; independent legitimate sources remain valid. Developer inspection cannot equip unavailable records.

Weapon filters use 13 actual database templates and game/mod localization labels, including one-handed spears, two-handed spears and staves. Off-hand weapon searches exclude two-handed weapons. A separate Awakened filter and optional character-level filter replace the hidden level-100 acquisition cutoff. All 126 source-backed Awakened records are retained. Two have level-101 requirements in the source data; visible does not mean usable by a level-100 character.

Learned skill damage

The Offense panel now includes learned attack skills and an expanded per-effect breakdown. Columns distinguish base, skill-modified, converted and character-scaled damage. Weapon portions, damage over time, learned modifiers, unlocked set modifiers, item modifiers, charge stacks and conversion paths are represented separately. Rank-indexed projectile references select only the current rank. Dual-wield users can preview each hand without summing the weapons into one hit.

Conversion follows skill modifiers, local conversion, global conversion of the remaining unconverted portion, then character bonuses. Competing conversions above 100% normalize; Elemental flat damage splits equally. Converted packets are never converted twice. Damage over time only follows conversion where a corresponding DoT type exists. See the official combat guide.

Scope and compatibility

These are static per-effect estimates, not total DPS or game-engine parity. Projectile overlap, proc probability, enemy defenses, critical hits, target-dependent current-health damage and added retaliation are not included in flat totals. Charge previews start at one stack. Unmet weapon requirements and missing linked records are flagged. The merged source lacks a Corpse Spiders projectile reference at an affected rank; its result is marked incomplete rather than replaced with guessed data.

Acquisition checks follow positive-weight database sources, crafting, vendors, quest roots and the GAL NPC exchange scripts. They do not simulate every map placement, runtime quest condition or random roll. Existing build records are not deleted on import, and no character saves or game databases are modified. DB1, schema v1, celestial rank policies, language memory, build metadata and legacy links remain compatible. English remains first in the repository README and release notes.

Wraithbound is not an alias of Spiritbound. GAL v97d replaces the original accessory record with Spiritbound and D3 skill bonuses. An outdated internal description does not prove that the original pet affix is available. The planner preserves current mod effects and does not fabricate or restore vanilla affixes. Weapon and caster-offhand variants are handled by their actual records.
