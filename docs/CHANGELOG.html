v1.8 · 2026-10-25

Catalogs and weapon families

Re-audited 145,867 merged database records. Compared with the v1.71 baseline, 1,237 catalog records are restored and 1,242 are now flagged unavailable in the normal catalog. This includes crafting skill runes and Eternal equipment outside the old records/items path restriction. Non-installable reward cards, crafting-only exchange materials, pet-only weapons, inactive affixes and cosmetic hairstyles are excluded from normal build selection. Creature equipment/loot references with dropItems=false are not acquisition sources; independent legitimate sources remain valid. Developer inspection cannot equip unavailable records.

Weapon filters use 13 actual database templates and game/mod localization labels, including one-handed spears, two-handed spears and staves. Off-hand weapon searches exclude two-handed weapons. A separate Awakened filter and optional character-level filter replace the hidden level-100 acquisition cutoff. All 126 source-backed Awakened records are retained. Two have level-101 requirements in the source data; visible does not mean usable by a level-100 character.

Learned skill damage

The Offense panel now includes learned attack skills and an expanded per-effect breakdown. Columns distinguish base, skill-modified, converted and character-scaled damage. Weapon portions, damage over time, learned modifiers, unlocked set modifiers, item modifiers, charge stacks and conversion paths are represented separately. Rank-indexed projectile references select only the current rank. Dual-wield users can preview each hand without summing the weapons into one hit.

Conversion follows skill modifiers, local conversion, global conversion of the remaining unconverted portion, then character bonuses. Competing conversions above 100% normalize; Elemental flat damage splits equally. Converted packets are never converted twice. Damage over time only follows conversion where a corresponding DoT type exists. See the official combat guide.

Scope and compatibility

These are static per-effect estimates, not total DPS or game-engine parity. Projectile overlap, proc probability, enemy defenses, critical hits, target-dependent current-health damage and added retaliation are not included in flat totals. Charge previews start at one stack. Unmet weapon requirements and missing linked records are flagged. The merged source lacks a Corpse Spiders projectile reference at an affected rank; its result is marked incomplete rather than replaced with guessed data.

Acquisition checks follow positive-weight database sources, crafting, vendors, quest roots and the GAL NPC exchange scripts. They do not simulate every map placement, runtime quest condition or random roll. Existing build records are not deleted on import, and no character saves or game databases are modified. DB1, schema v1, celestial rank policies, language memory, build metadata and legacy links remain compatible. English remains first in the repository README and release notes.

简体中文 · v1.8 · 2026-10-25

装备目录与武器分类

重新扫描145,867条合并数据库记录。相对v1.71基线，补回1,237条目录记录，将1,242条记录标记为不纳入正常目录，包括修复records/items路径限制误排除的制作技能符文、永恒之境装备等。奖励兑换卡牌、仅作制作材料的道具、宠物专用武器、无有效词缀生成池和外观发型不进入普通构筑选择；明确dropItems=false的生物装备/掉落引用不作为来源；物品另有独立有效获取来源时保留。开发者可查看但不可选装不可用条目。

按13种真实武器模板和游戏/模组汉化标签筛选，包含单手长矛、双手长矛及法杖。副手武器不显示双手类型。新增“仅觉醒”和独立等级筛选，不再将100级上限混入获取性判定。126条有来源的觉醒装备均保留；两件数据库需求等级为101，显示并不表示100级角色可以装备。

已学技能伤害

角色攻击面板增加已学攻击技能及展开明细，分别显示基础、技能修正后、转换后、人物加成后。武器部分、持续伤害、已学进阶、生效套装修正、物品修正、蓄能层数和转换路径分开展示。等级索引弹体只读取当前等级对应记录。双持可切换单手预览，不将两把武器相加为一次命中。

按技能修正、技能内转换、剩余未转换部分的全局转换、人物加成处理；超过100%的竞争转换按比例归一，元素固定伤害均分三系，不进行二次转换。持续伤害仅在目标存在对应持续伤害类型时联动转换。

范围与兼容

这是单次效果静态预估，不是实战总DPS或游戏引擎完全还原。弹体重叠、触发概率、敌人防御、暴击、依赖目标当前生命的伤害和反击附加不计入固定伤害合计。蓄能预览默认一层。武器条件不满足、关联记录缺失会明确提示。合并原始库中巫医尸蛛某等级弹体引用缺失，按不完整显示，不拿其他等级的效果代替。

获取性按正权重来源、制作、商店、任务根记录及GAL转换脚本复核，未模拟全部地图实体放置、运行时任务条件和随机变量。导入不会删除旧构筑记录，不修改角色存档或游戏库。DB1、schema v1、星座等级导入策略、语言记忆、构筑资料与旧链接保留兼容。

v1.71 · 2026-10-20

星座触发技能支持自定义等级，默认满级，可一键回满；统一读取自身或关联效果的经验成长表，不再把星点投入上限1误作技能成长上限。技能说明、触发增益、召唤物和当前构筑削弱同步使用所选等级。导入构筑文件、分享码或分享链接默认满级；导入游戏角色.gdc按存档当前等级。本地保存、重启恢复、撤销和重做保留手动等级。语言偏好独立保存到本机，启动时恢复。

Celestial powers now have editable ranks, defaulting to maximum, with a one-click maximum command. Growth caps are resolved from the power or its linked effect, not the one-point allocation cap. Tooltips, triggered buffs, summons and build debuffs share the selected rank. Build-file/code/link imports default to maximum; native .gdc imports retain the character's current ranks. Local saves, session restoration, undo and redo retain custom ranks. Language is persisted independently and restored on launch.

v1.6 核查说明 / Verified behavior

Wraithbound并非Spiritbound的别名。两份最新汉化均将Wraithbound译为“幽灵束缚”。GAL v97d将原版饰品对应记录覆盖为Spiritbound / 灵魂束缚，属性改为D3技能加成；旧内部备注仍为Wraithbound，但不能作为名称或战宠属性的证据。本工具保留当前模组实际数据，不修改游戏或伪造被替换的原版词缀。武器与法器版本分别按实际记录筛选。

Wraithbound is not an alias of Spiritbound. GAL v97d replaces the original accessory record with Spiritbound and D3 skill bonuses. An outdated internal description does not prove that the original pet affix is available. The planner preserves current mod effects and does not fabricate or restore vanilla affixes. Weapon and caster-offhand variants are handled by their actual records.

飞升抗性：新增独立飞升分类，按终极难度抗性及独立飞升敌人调整计算。当前本机v1.3.1.1 + GAL v97d中，通用飞升调整对列出的十种伤害抗性均为+0；生命、伤害、速度和攻防能力依然有变化。随机装备、临时技能、祭坛供奉、碎裂层数及场地变异不在该基础统计范围。

Ascendant resistances: calculated from Ultimate plus its separate Ascendant adjustment. The installed v1.3.1.1 + GAL v97d dataset adds +0 to all ten listed general resistances, while other monster stats still change. Random gear, temporary effects, offerings, shard scaling and mutators remain outside this base comparison.

数值范围：装备变量仍为近似浮动模型。关联触发技能及人物伤害加成召唤物补充基础效果和固定伤害静态预估；武器伤害部分、反击附加、敌人减伤、暴击、特殊机制及实际触发覆盖率尚未全部建模。对敌削弱显示可同时施加的潜力，不等于常驻覆盖率；低于100%武器伤害的技能须折算武器降抗效果。不能据此宣称角色面板已与游戏引擎完全一致。

Numerical scope: item rolls remain approximate. Linked procs and player-scaling summons expose their base effects and estimated scaled flat damage, not a complete engine simulation. Weapon-damage portions, added retaliation, enemy mitigation, crits, special mechanics and uptime are not fully modeled. Build debuffs show potential simultaneous effects; weapon-hit effects require scaling below 100% weapon damage.

额外数据核查：41处原始技能修正引用不存在，已在查询页单列，未编造效果。Additional audit: 41 modifier references are absent from the merged source database. They are listed separately; no effects are invented.
